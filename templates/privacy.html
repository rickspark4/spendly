Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register. We also collect the expense records you log, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions, in order to power the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate and improve Spendly — authenticating your account, storing your expense history, generating spending summaries and category breakdowns, and communicating with you about your account. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal or financial data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and email delivery services) to operate the Service, and these providers only process data as needed to provide their service to us.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
